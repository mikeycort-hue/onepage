Quick tips

  • Type a thought and press Return — it goes on top and the keyboard stays up.
  • Start a line with "x " to make it a to-do.
  • Swipe right on a thought to open its sub-thoughts; swipe left for Style, Remind and Delete.
  • Pull down to search. Swipe in from the right edge for the Time Log.
  • Shake to undo. Deleted thoughts stay in Settings › Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Backups are in the Files app under On My iPhone › OnePage › Backups.
