OnePage Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

OnePage is a notes app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: nothing leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. OnePage has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. The app makes no network connections.

Where your notes are stored

Everything you write — your thoughts, sub-thoughts, reminders, the Time Log and backups — is stored only on your iPhone, inside OnePage's own storage. Backup files are saved to OnePage's folder in the Files app on your device.

If you have turned on iCloud Backup for your iPhone, Apple includes OnePage's data in your device backup, as it does for any app. That is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings; OnePage itself never uploads anything.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Widgets and controls

The Home Screen widget shows your newest thoughts. It reads them from storage shared only between OnePage and its own widget, on your device.

Sharing

Your notes only leave the app if you choose to export them using the share sheet, and only to the place you choose.

Children

OnePage collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if an optional sync feature is added — the change will be described here and in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
